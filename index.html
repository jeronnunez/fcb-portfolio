About Me

My expertise includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, data visualization, statistical analysis, and reporting to identify trends, patterns, and opportunities. Using analytical tools and data-driven techniques, I aim to simplify complex information and present actionable findings through clear reports and interactive dashboards. I am committed to continuous learning and applying data analytics to solve real-world problems and help organizations make smarter, evidence-based decisions.
